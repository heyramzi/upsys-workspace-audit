# Interview mode: eight questions

Ask two or three at a time, in this order. Take a rough answer; "about a third" is enough. If they don't know, mark the check it feeds Not measured.

| # | Question | Feeds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | What's the agency called, how many people deliver client work, how many clients are active, and who plans the team's week? | Capacity, structure, who does each fix |
| 2 | What spaces do you have in ClickUp, and where does a new client project go? How many statuses does that list have? | Structure |
| 3 | Roughly what share of open tasks have nobody assigned, or more than one person? | Ownership |
| 4 | Do tasks carry any effort number, like points or a time estimate? On roughly what share? | Effort |
| 5 | When someone opens ClickUp on Monday, how do they know what's theirs for this week? And roughly what share of open work has a date in the next two weeks? | Weekly planning |
| 6 | How does your busiest person's week look right now? Tasks due, or points or hours if you track them. | Capacity |
| 7 | On a normal Friday, roughly what share of what was planned for the week is still open? | Slippage |
| 8 | About how many tasks are overdue right now? | Slippage |

After the last answer, play back the six checks in one short list ("Ownership: about 20% unassigned...") and ask if it's right before writing the report.

In the report, mark every score in interview mode "from your answers" so nobody mistakes it for measured data.

If they ask for a fix before the interview's done, give the short steps from the fix recipes in report.md, say it'll be ranked in the report, and carry on with the next questions.

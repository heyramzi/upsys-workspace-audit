# Live mode: what to read

Use only the read and search tools the connected ClickUp app gives you. Tool names differ between versions of the app, so pick by what a tool does, not its name. If a tool can't return something below, mark the checks that need it Not measured.

## Order

1. **Workspaces and spaces.** List them. If there's more than one workspace, ask which. Ask which space holds client delivery unless one is plainly named for it (Delivery, Clients, Projects, Production).
2. **Folders and lists** in that space. Note list names and the statuses on the two or three busiest lists.
3. **Custom fields.** Look for a BATCH or batch-style week field, a Points or effort field, a client field, and time estimates.
4. **Members.** Who is on the team. Leave out guests and bots from capacity.
5. **Tasks.** Open tasks in the delivery space, with subtasks, including: assignees, status, start and due dates, the batch and points fields, time estimate, tags. Then tasks closed in the last 14 days with their closed date, to measure last week's batch. If the tools don't return a closed date, say so and use the due-date window in scoring.md.

Read in pages if the app pages results. Stop at 500 open tasks and say the audit covers a sample.

## Before scoring, tell them what you read

One line, with numbers: "I read the Delivery space: 4 folders, 19 lists, 212 open tasks, 9 people. No BATCH field; a Story Points field on 3 lists." Ask them to correct anything wrong before you score.

## Privacy

Don't repeat task descriptions, comments or client details in the report beyond what a finding needs. Name a task only when it's the example behind a fix.
